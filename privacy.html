Comedy Show Finder – Privacy Policy

Comedy Show Finder is a small hobby app for finding comedy shows in New York City.

What we collect

If you sign in, we store your email address, the username you choose, a salted hash of your password (if you use email sign-in), and the ratings, favorites, shows attended, friends and invites you add. If you sign in with Google, we receive only your email address; we do not request any other Google data.

How it is used

Only to run the app: keep you signed in, show your history, and let friends you approve see it. We do not sell or share your data and do not show ads.

Storage

Data is kept in a private Google Sheet owned by the app's developer. Your sign-in is remembered in your browser so you don't have to log in each time; logging out clears it.

Deleting your data

Email lejuez88@gmail.com and your account and data will be removed.
